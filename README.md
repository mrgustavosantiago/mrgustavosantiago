### 🧑‍💻 What I actually do

- 🏗️ **Architecture & software analysis** — I like systems that are boring in production and interesting in the design doc, not the other way around.
- 💸 **Payments at scale** — PIX, boleto, TED and EDI integrations with Brazil's largest banks, handling **millions of requests a day**. Money moving correctly is a feature, not a bonus.
- 🤖 **AI-powered product features** — because "just add AI" is a requirement I hear a lot, and someone has to make it actually work.
- 🧪 **Tests I trust** — RSpec, Cucumber, Cypress, Playwright. If it isn't tested, it's just a rumor.
- 🧠 **Collaborative teams** — I do my best work where knowledge flows freely and someone brings a genuinely hard problem to the table.
